===== LUM-1332 — composer drag-select + copy-on-select (codex chat_composer/mouse.rs `copy_selection`, upstream pi-ts screen selection semantics), real binary + real PTY + pyte. Panel 1: dragging from the first 'l' of 'llo' to the 'o' of 'world' highlights exactly those characters (reversed video) and moves the caret to the drag's end. Panel 2: the release copies exactly that text — the '# clipboard' line is the OSC 52 payload the terminal received, and the frame is unchanged because a copy is not an edit. Panel 3: a plain click still places the caret and copies nothing (LUM-1327, unchanged). Panels 4-5: a drag across the line break selects through it and copies 'irst line\nseco'. Panels 6-7: a drag that leaves the composer clamps to the composer's edge — the transcript above shows no highlight at all — its release copies the clamped fragment ('line\ns'), and a click on the status bar dismisses the highlight without copying anything (a press elsewhere is a new gesture). Panels 9-10 repeat it over CJK text, where a glyph owns two cells (LUM-1336's column measure): the five highlighted cells are 你 + caret + 好, and the copy is those two glyphs.
